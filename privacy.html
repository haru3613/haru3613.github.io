隱私權政策 / Privacy Policy

最後更新 / Last updated: 2026-09-25

用途 / Purpose

Haru Video Studio 僅供獲授權的頻道管理者使用。Google OAuth 連結 YouTube 頻道，讓工具確認頻道身分、上傳影片，以及查核上傳或排程結果。

The tool is for an authorized channel owner. Google OAuth connects the owner's YouTube channel so the tool can verify channel identity, upload videos, and check upload or scheduling results.

存取資料 / Data accessed

工具使用 youtube.readonly 讀取所連頻道的身分、上傳清單、影片 ID、標題與發布狀態，用於目標核對、避免重複上傳及失敗復原。工具使用 youtube.upload 將管理者選定的影片、封面及中繼資料送往 YouTube。它不要求 Gmail、聯絡人或 Google 雲端硬碟權限。

Read access is used for channel identity, upload lists, video IDs, titles, and publication status to verify the target and avoid duplicate uploads. Upload access sends owner-selected video files, thumbnails, and metadata to YouTube. The tool does not request Gmail, Contacts, or Drive access.

儲存與分享 / Storage and sharing

OAuth 權杖保存在管理者 Mac 的受保護本機目錄，不寫入 Git、公開網頁或影片。製作與發布收據留在本機供查核與復原。只有管理者授權上傳時，影片及中繼資料才送往 YouTube；工具不出售 Google 使用者資料，也不用它投放廣告。

OAuth tokens stay in protected local storage on the owner's Mac, outside Git, public pages, and videos. Local production and publication receipts support audit and recovery. Videos and metadata go to YouTube when an authorized upload occurs. Google user data is not sold or used for advertising.

保留與撤銷 / Retention and revocation

本機憑證與收據保留到管理者刪除或撤銷授權。管理者可在 Google 帳戶的第三方連結設定撤銷存取，並可來信要求說明或刪除本機資料。撤銷本機排程不會自動取消已在 YouTube 排定的影片；需在 YouTube 另行檢查。

Local credentials and receipts remain until the owner removes them or revokes access. The owner can revoke the Google grant in their account settings and contact us about local data removal. Stopping the local schedule does not automatically cancel videos already scheduled on YouTube.
