隱私權政策 / Privacy Policy

最後更新 / Last updated: 2026-10-09

用途 / Purpose

Haru Video Studio 僅供獲授權的管理者使用。Google Drive OAuth 用於管理製片素材與交付檔案；若管理者另外連結 YouTube，才會使用原有的頻道核對及核准上傳功能。

Haru Video Studio is used only by its authorized owner. Google Drive OAuth is used to manage production assets and delivered files. Existing channel verification and approved-upload features are used only if the owner separately connects YouTube.

存取資料 / Data accessed

新增 drive.file 權限，僅可查看、建立、修改或刪除此應用建立或管理者選取的特定 Drive 檔案。本次搬移使用它上傳及校驗素材與成片；搬移工具不刪除 Drive 檔案。工具不要求 Gmail 或聯絡人權限。原有 YouTube 權限及發布控制維持獨立。

The added drive.file permission can access, create, modify, or delete only specific Drive files created by this app or selected by the owner. The current migration uses it to upload and verify production assets and finished videos; the migration tool does not delete Drive files. The tool does not request Gmail or Contacts permissions. Existing YouTube permissions and publishing controls remain separate.

儲存與分享 / Storage and sharing

OAuth 憑證保存在管理者電腦的受保護目錄，或管理者部署的私有雲端服務設定與受保護目錄；不寫入 Git、公開網頁、影片或一般操作紀錄。製作收據與專案資料可保存在本機或私有雲端，素材與交付物可保存在管理者自己的 Drive。只有執行管理者要求的製片工作時，才將必要內容送往已啟用的製片供應商。工具不出售 Google 使用者資料，也不用它投放廣告。

OAuth credentials are stored in protected directories on the owner's computer or in the configuration and protected directories of the owner's privately deployed cloud service. They are not written to Git, public webpages, videos, or general operation logs. Production receipts and project data may be stored locally or in the private cloud, while assets and deliverables may be stored in the owner's Drive. Necessary content is sent to enabled production providers only when performing work requested by the owner. The tool does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

保留、刪除與撤銷 / Retention, deletion, and revocation

本機／雲端憑證與製作紀錄保留至管理者刪除或撤銷授權。管理者可透過 Google 帳戶的第三方連結設定撤銷存取。撤銷授權不會自動刪除 Drive 中的素材，也不會刪除已上傳至 YouTube 的影片。資料刪除聯絡地址維持 qaw8975889@gmail.com。

Local and cloud credentials and production records remain until the owner deletes them or revokes authorization. The owner can revoke access through Google Account third-party connection settings. Revoking authorization does not automatically delete assets in Drive or videos already uploaded to YouTube. The data deletion contact remains qaw8975889@gmail.com.

Google 另依其隱私權政策 / Privacy Policy處理 Google 服務中的資料。
