使用資格 / Authorized use

Haru Video Studio 目前僅供對所連 YouTube 頻道具有管理權限的人使用。使用者須確認對上傳的影片、聲音、封面及文字具有必要權利，並遵守 YouTube 的規則。

The tool is currently for people authorized to manage the connected YouTube channel. The operator is responsible for rights to uploaded video, audio, thumbnails, and text, and for following YouTube's rules.

發布控制 / Publishing control

製片與上傳受本機品質檢查、活動範圍及發布授權限制。自動流程可能因網路、額度、憑證或平台拒絕而跳過某一天，不保證每日一定公開。已在 YouTube 排程的影片須於 YouTube 管理。

Local quality checks, campaign scope, and publication authorization govern production and upload. Network, quota, credential, or platform failures may cause a day to be skipped. Videos already scheduled on YouTube must be managed there.
